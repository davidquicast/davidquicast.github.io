# Read.cv reference catalog

This catalog records the 47 visual references in the [supplied recreated Read.cv Figma kit](https://www.figma.com/design/kxi9jO6ZIUzU2Dmcu6Lm6q/Read.cv-UI---Free-UI-Kit--Recreated---Community-?node-id=0-1). The approved product remains a personal static portfolio. Reference account, billing and social states informed interface analysis; they do not introduce registration, publishing or a new backend.

## Evidence and limits

All three editable frames were opened as exact-node screenshots. All 44 raster assets were opened across eight overview sheets. Twenty-two full-resolution raster originals, usually about 2880 px wide, were paired with those overviews and visually checked. Two further raster states have full MCP node exports. Twenty raster states remain overview-only. Raster IDs below are stable catalog IDs, not asserted Figma node IDs; exact node correspondence is established privately for nine rasters, not all 44.

Figma MCP was genuinely used for metadata, editable design context, screenshots and ten successful exports. Its Starter tool-call limit prevented further MCP exports. The ordinary public viewer then supplied the raster cache without account changes, purchases or credential extraction. Raw captures, temporary asset URLs and diagnostic logs remain private and excluded from the public build.

Static captures establish composition and visible states. They do not establish transition duration, runtime behavior or accessibility. Those behaviors are implemented and checked separately in the portfolio.

## Design decisions carried forward

The kit uses Inter Regular and Medium; that does not establish the original service’s font license. The portfolio self-hosts Inter and does not copy Graphik binaries. The measured editable profile uses 20px name text, 14px interface text, a 92px avatar and a 604px primary column. Compact text, quiet neutral surfaces, chronology and real images form the hierarchy. The portfolio adds explicit navigation labels because destination clarity matters on a personal site.

The implemented light and dark palettes, focus/contrast adjustments, responsive rules, typography, Pretext composition and native motion are documented in [the current design specification](readcv.md). Source screenshots with missing black video areas or dimmed intermediate states are not final layout targets. Original project media, article routes and real owner information remain the content foundation.
