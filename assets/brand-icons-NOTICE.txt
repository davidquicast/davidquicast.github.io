Official multicolor Hugging Face asset
-------------------------------------
assets/icons/huggingface-color.svg is the original Hugging Face brand asset,
copyright Hugging Face. Downloaded from the official asset link supplied at
https://huggingface.co/brand for project use:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/huggingface/brand-assets/resolve/main/hf-logo.svg
The asset is preserved byte-for-byte, separate from Simple Icons' CC0 paths.
No open-source license is asserted for this trademark asset. Hugging Face
retains its rights. Official palette: #FFD21E and #FF9D00.

Brand-color metadata is from the same pinned Simple Icons snapshot under
CC0; LinkedIn uses its official #0A66C2. GitHub and X use monochrome marks
with theme-aware black/white foregrounds. Other icons remain currentColor
unless the include is explicitly requested with colored=true.
